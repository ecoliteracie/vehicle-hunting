## Powertrain

Both trims use a 192-hp, 192-lb-ft turbocharged 1.5-liter four-cylinder, continuously variable transmission, and front-wheel drive. AWD is not offered. Normal and ECON drive modes plus idle-stop are standard.

## Universal standard equipment

- Honda Sensing: Collision Mitigation Braking, Road Departure Mitigation, adaptive cruise control with Low-Speed Follow, Lane Keeping Assist, Traffic Jam Assist, and Traffic Sign Recognition.
- 10.2-inch digital instrument cluster, 9-inch touchscreen, wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, wireless phone charging, four-speaker audio, and front USB-C ports.
- Full LED headlights, push-button start, automatic climate control, and a multi-angle rearview camera.

## Grade-by-grade buying path

- LX to SE: keeps the same 192-hp engine and adds heated front seats, 10-way power driver seat, moonroof, dual-zone climate control, Smart Entry/remote start, Blind Spot Information/Cross Traffic Monitor, larger 19-inch wheels, and upgraded 8-speaker audio.

## Quick buying guide

- **LX:** the value choice; it keeps the same engine, digital gauges, core safety suite, and best gas-model fuel economy.
- **SE:** the worthwhile comfort upgrade if you want heated seats, a power driver seat, moonroof, blind-spot monitoring, larger wheels, and keyless-entry conveniences.
- Choose the separately covered **Accord Hybrid** if acceleration, a 12.3-inch touchscreen, or substantially better city economy matters; no gas Accord trim offers AWD.
