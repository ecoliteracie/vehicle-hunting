# 2027 Honda CR-V Hybrid (U.S.) - Trim & Feature Comparison

> **Scope:** 2027 CR-V two-motor hybrid trims. **S** = standard; **A** = available; **-** = not offered. MSRP excludes destination, taxes, registration, accessories, and dealer markup.

## Lineup, drivetrain, and efficiency

| Trim | Base MSRP / drive | EPA MPG (city/hwy/combined) | Positioning |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Sport Hybrid | $36,030 / FWD | 43 / 36 / 40 | Entry hybrid |
| TrailSport Hybrid | $39,200 / AWD | 38 / 33 / 35 | Rugged/all-terrain |
| Sport-L Hybrid | $39,125 / FWD | 43 / 36 / 40 | Leather sport trim |
| Sport Touring Hybrid | $42,950 / AWD | 40 / 34 / 37 | Top luxury/technology |

Sport Hybrid and Sport-L Hybrid offer Real Time AWD for $1,500; their AWD rating is 40/34/37 MPG. TrailSport and Sport Touring are AWD-only.

## Powertrain and capability

All use Honda's two-motor hybrid system with a 2.0-liter Atkinson-cycle engine: 204 total system hp and 247 lb-ft of peak traction-motor torque. Sport, TrailSport, and Sport-L have ECON, Normal, Snow, and Sport modes; Sport Touring adds Individual mode. Hybrid towing capacity is 1,000 lb when properly equipped.

## Universal standard equipment

- Honda Sensing, Blind Spot Information with Cross Traffic Monitor, full LED lighting, and roof rails.
- 9-inch touchscreen, wireless Apple CarPlay/Android Auto, wireless charging, and remote start.
- Heated front seats, dual-zone climate control, moonroof, 10-way power driver seat, and power tailgate.

## Quick buying guide

- **Sport Hybrid:** best-value hybrid and best FWD efficiency.
- **TrailSport Hybrid:** standard AWD, all-terrain tires, unique suspension tuning/styling, heated wheel, and TrailSport cabin details; it has the lineup's lowest MPG.
- **Sport-L Hybrid:** leather, memory, power passenger seat, and parking aids without forcing AWD.
- **Sport Touring Hybrid:** standard AWD plus the quietest/premium specification, Google built-in, Bose audio, hands-free tailgate, 19-inch wheels, and Individual mode.
