# 2026 Toyota Corolla (U.S.) - Trim & Feature Comparison

> **Scope:** Corolla sedan for the U.S. market, model year 2026. This is a shopper-friendly comparison of standard equipment and major trim differences. **S** = standard; **A** = available as an option/package; **-** = not offered on that trim. Pricing, packages, colors, and availability can vary by region and change during the model year.

## Powertrain and performance

| System | Trims | Engine / transmission | Output | Drive layout |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| Gas | LE, SE, XSE | 2.0L four-cylinder; Dynamic-Shift CVT | 169 hp; 151 lb-ft | FWD only |
| Hybrid | Hybrid LE, Hybrid SE, Hybrid XLE | 1.8L four-cylinder Toyota Hybrid System; ECVT | 138 net combined hp; 105 lb-ft | FWD standard; Electronic On-Demand AWD available on Hybrid LE and Hybrid SE only |

- The **Hybrid XLE is FWD only**. Hybrid LE/SE AWD are distinct configurations, with their own lower EPA ratings.
- Gas LE uses Normal and Eco drive modes. The SE, Hybrid SE, and XSE use Normal, Sport, and Eco; Hybrid LE and Hybrid XLE use Normal and Eco.

## Universal standard equipment

Every listed Corolla sedan trim includes:

- Toyota Safety Sense 3.0, including Pre-Collision System with Pedestrian Detection, Full-Speed Range Dynamic Radar Cruise Control, Lane Departure Alert with Steering Assist, Lane Tracing Assist, Road Sign Assist, Automatic High Beams, and Proactive Driving Assist.
- Blind Spot Monitor with Rear Cross-Traffic Alert and ten airbags.
- An 8-inch Toyota Audio Multimedia touchscreen (the XSE upgrades to 10.5 inches), six speakers, wireless Apple CarPlay and wireless Android Auto, SiriusXM trial, and front and rear USB-C ports.
- FWD, electric power steering, four-wheel disc brakes, and a 5-passenger sedan body.
- Toyota connected-services capability: Remote Connect, Safety Connect, Drive Connect, Service Connect, and Wi-Fi Connect. Trials/subscriptions and cellular coverage apply.

## What changes as you move up

- **LE to Hybrid LE:** swaps the 2.0L gas drivetrain for the 1.8L hybrid system, adds the option of AWD, and improves fuel economy substantially.
- **LE / Hybrid LE to SE / Hybrid SE:** moves toward a sport-focused setup: Sport mode, sport seats, 18-inch wheels, and multi-link rear suspension.
- **SE to XSE:** retains the gas sport setup and adds the 12.3-inch gauge cluster, 10.5-inch screen, Smart Key, heated SofTex sport seats, standard moonroof, and available JBL audio.
- **Hybrid SE to Hybrid XLE:** trades the sport emphasis for premium/comfort equipment: 12.3-inch gauge cluster, heated SofTex seats, standard moonroof, and available JBL audio. Hybrid XLE is FWD only.

## Package / shopping cautions

- An **A** in the table does not mean it will be on every dealer vehicle; verify the window sticker or build sheet.
- Toyota lists the **LE Premium Package** and **SE Premium Package**. These can change individual vehicle equipment such as Smart Key, moonroof, and larger multimedia displays.
- Fuel-economy figures are EPA estimates; AWD hybrid figures are lower than their FWD counterparts.
- MSRP excludes delivery, processing/handling, taxes, title/license, dealer-installed accessories, and dealer markup.
