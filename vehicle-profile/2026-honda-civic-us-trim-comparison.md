| Trim | Powertrain / drive | Base MSRP | EPA MPG (city/hwy/combined) | Positioning |

| LX | 2.0L + CVT, FWD | $24,695 | 32 / 41 / 36 | Lowest price / best gas MPG |

Both use a naturally aspirated 2.0-liter four-cylinder producing 150 hp and 133 lb-ft. AWD and a manual transmission are not offered on these trims.

## Universal standard equipment

- Honda Sensing with adaptive cruise control with Low-Speed Follow, Collision Mitigation Braking, Road Departure Mitigation, Lane Keeping Assist, and Traffic Sign Recognition.
- 7-inch touchscreen, wired Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, 7-inch driver display, push-button start, and automatic climate control.
- Full LED headlights and a multi-angle rearview camera with dynamic guidelines.

## Grade-by-grade buying path

- LX to Sport: keeps the same 150-hp engine and fuel economy, and adds 18-inch gloss-black wheels, CVT paddle shifters, additional drive modes, leather-wrapped steering wheel, Smart Entry/remote start, Blind Spot Information/Cross Traffic Monitor, and heated side mirrors.

## Quick buying guide

- **LX:** best value and fuel economy; choose it if the smaller wheels and basic audio/entry system are acceptable.
- **Sport:** adds the useful blind-spot system and keyless/remote-start features, plus larger black wheels and sport styling. It does not add horsepower.
- The separately covered **Sport Hybrid** costs more but delivers 200 system hp and 49 combined MPG; **Civic Si** is the manual-transmission enthusiast model.
