## Universal standard equipment

Every 2026 Camry includes:

- 2.5L four-cylinder fifth-generation Toyota Hybrid System: 225 net combined hp with FWD or 232 net combined hp with AWD.
- Electronic On-Demand AWD is available on every trim; all have Normal, Eco, and Sport drive modes.
- Toyota Safety Sense 3.0, Blind Spot Monitor, Rear Cross-Traffic Alert, Safe Exit Alert, LED headlights/DRLs, and a backup camera.
- Push-button start, dual-zone automatic climate control with rear vents, Qi wireless charging, and five USB ports.
- Wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, dual Bluetooth phone connectivity, and six speakers.

## Premium Plus Package (XLE / XSE only)

When equipped, it adds Traffic Jam Assist, Front Cross-Traffic Alert, Lane Change Assist, Panoramic View Monitor, Front and Rear Parking Assist with Automatic Braking, driver-seat/side-mirror memory, and automatic windshield wipers. Digital Key requires the applicable package and an active Remote Connect trial or subscription.

## Quick buying guide

- **LE:** best economy and lowest entry price; the only 51-combined-MPG Camry in FWD form.
- **SE:** the practical sport trim with 18-inch black wheels, sport seating, and sport-tuned suspension.
- **Nightshade:** essentially a black-accented sport model, with exclusive 19-inch satin-black wheels, black grille/badging, and black spoiler.
- **XLE:** premium and quieter ride focus, with 12.3-inch displays, heated leather/microfiber seating, and upscale options.
- **XSE:** combines the higher-end technology with sport suspension, aggressive exterior styling, and 19-inch wheels.
