## Powertrain

Both trims pair a 2.0-liter Atkinson-cycle four-cylinder with a two-motor hybrid system for 200 total system hp. The traction motor produces 232 lb-ft from 0-2,000 rpm. Both are FWD-only and include regenerative-braking deceleration selectors; AWD is not available.

## Universal standard equipment

- Honda Sensing plus Blind Spot Information with Cross Traffic Monitor.
- 18-inch alloy wheels, heated front seats, one-touch moonroof, dual-zone climate control, and a 60/40 split rear seat.
- 7-inch touchscreen with wired Apple CarPlay/Android Auto, eight-speaker audio, Smart Entry, and remote start.

## Grade-by-grade buying path

- Sport Hybrid to Sport Touring Hybrid: keeps the same 200-hp hybrid system and EPA rating, and adds leather seating, an 8-way power driver seat, larger 9-inch touchscreen and 10.2-inch digital cluster, wireless phone integration, Google built-in, Bose 12-speaker audio, a wireless charger, and parking sensors.

## Quick buying guide

- **Sport Hybrid:** keeps the same power, fuel economy, heated seats, moonroof, and safety fundamentals for $3,000 less.
- **Sport Touring Hybrid:** the technology/luxury step—leather, power driver seat, larger displays, wireless phone integration, Google built-in, Bose audio, and parking aids.
- Neither sedan hybrid offers AWD. Shoppers needing a liftgate can compare the separate Civic Hatchback Hybrid lineup.
