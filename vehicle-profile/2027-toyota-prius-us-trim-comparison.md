## Powertrain and performance

| System | Engine / transmission | Output | Drivetrain availability |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Fifth-generation Toyota Hybrid System | 2.0L four-cylinder hybrid; ECVT | 194 net combined hp FWD; 196 net combined hp AWD | FWD and Electronic On-Demand AWD on every grade |

- Toyota quotes 0-60 mph in 7.2 seconds for FWD and 7.0 seconds for AWD.
- Every grade offers EV, Power, Eco, and Normal drive modes. EV mode is for short, low-speed operation when conditions allow.
- AWD costs $1,400 versus the corresponding FWD grade and reduces the EPA rating; it is not a separate trim equipment package.

## Universal standard equipment

Every 2027 Prius includes:

- Fifth-generation Toyota Hybrid System with a 2.0L four-cylinder engine: 194 net combined hp FWD, or 196 net combined hp AWD.
- EV, Power, Eco, and Normal drive modes; 60/40 split folding rear seats; and a rear liftback cargo opening.
- Toyota Safety Sense 3.0, Blind Spot Monitor with Rear Cross-Traffic Alert, and a 7-inch top-mount digital gauge display.
- Wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto compatibility, an 8-inch Toyota Audio Multimedia display, and Toyota connected-services capability.

## Quick buying guide

- **LE:** lowest price and best efficiency; its FWD 59/50 MPG rating is the range-focused choice.
- **XLE:** adds the key daily-comfort upgrades: 19-inch wheels, heated power SofTex seats, Qi charging, and rain-sensing wipers.
- **Nightshade:** the XLE equipment direction with black 19-inch wheels, dark exterior details, black/gray SofTex seating, and carbon-fiber-look trim.
- **Limited:** adds the premium technology and convenience equipment: 12.3-inch touchscreen, JBL audio, fixed glass roof, power liftback, driver memory, and ventilated front seats.
- **AWD:** available on every grade; it adds traction and 2 hp, but reduces fuel economy and adds $1,400 to the equivalent FWD grade.
