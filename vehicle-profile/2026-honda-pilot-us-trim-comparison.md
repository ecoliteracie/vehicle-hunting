Real Time i-VTM4 AWD is available on Sport, EX-L, and Touring Blackout; it is standard on TrailSport, Touring, Elite, and Black Edition. Verify the window sticker because FWD/AWD availability materially affects price, MPG, and towing.

## Powertrain, MPG, and capability

All trims use a 285-hp, 262-lb-ft 3.5-liter V6 and 10-speed automatic. FWD models are rated 19/27/22 MPG; most AWD models are 19/25/21. TrailSport is 18/23/20. AWD Pilots can tow up to 5,000 lb when properly equipped; FWD models are limited to 3,500 lb.

## Universal standard equipment

- Three rows, up to eight seats, tri-zone climate control, heated front seats, power driver seat, power tailgate, and roof rails.
- 12.3-inch touchscreen with Google built-in, wireless Apple CarPlay/Android Auto, Wi-Fi hotspot capability, and 10.2-inch digital gauges.
- Honda Sensing, Blind Spot Information with Cross Traffic Monitor, rearview camera, and Hill Descent Control.

## Quick buying guide

- **Sport:** strong standard technology but cloth seating and a non-removable second-row bench.
- **EX-L:** value sweet spot for families wanting leather, the stowable center seat, parking sensors, and wireless charging.
- **TrailSport:** choose for actual hardware—standard AWD with Trail Torque Logic, all-terrain tires, off-road suspension, skid plates, recovery points, hitch, and TrailWatch.
- **Touring:** the road-focused premium choice with Bose audio, panoramic roof, multi-view camera, and hands-free tailgate.
- **Elite / Black Edition:** add the comfort flagship features; Black Edition is primarily an appearance treatment over Elite.
