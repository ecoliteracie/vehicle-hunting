# 2027 Toyota Corolla (U.S.) - Trim Comparison

> U.S. Corolla sedan. S = standard; A = available; - = not offered.

## Trims

| Trim | Powertrain / drive | MSRP | Est. MPG city/hwy |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| LE | 2.0L gas, FWD | $23,325 | 32 / 41 |
| Hybrid LE | 1.8L hybrid, FWD; AWD available | $25,175 | 53 / 46 |
| SE | 2.0L gas, FWD | $25,765 | 31 / 40 |
| Hybrid SE | 1.8L hybrid, FWD; AWD available | $27,615 | 50 / 43 |
| XSE | 2.0L gas, FWD | $29,040 | 31 / 38 |
| Hybrid XLE | 1.8L hybrid, FWD | $29,540 | 53 / 46 |

AWD Hybrid LE is $26,575 (51 / 44 MPG); AWD Hybrid SE is $29,015 (47 / 41 MPG).

## Powertrain and performance

| System | Trims | Engine / transmission | Output | Drive layout |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| Gas | LE, SE, XSE | 2.0L Dynamic Force four-cylinder; Dynamic-Shift CVT | 169 hp; 151 lb-ft | FWD only |
| Hybrid | Hybrid LE, Hybrid SE, Hybrid XLE | 1.8L four-cylinder Toyota Hybrid System 5; ECVT | 138 net combined hp | FWD standard; Electronic On-Demand AWD available on Hybrid LE and Hybrid SE only |

- **Hybrid XLE is FWD only.** AWD Hybrid LE/SE variants are separately priced and have lower EPA ratings.
- The gas CVT uses a physical launch gear for initial acceleration; XSE also has paddle shifters for 10 preset ratios.
- Drive modes vary by grade. The sport grades receive Normal, Sport, and Eco modes; LE-oriented grades prioritize Normal and Eco modes.

## Standard on every regular trim

- Toyota Safety Sense 3.0 and Blind Spot Monitor with Rear Cross-Traffic Alert.
- Wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto.
- 8-inch Toyota Audio Multimedia touchscreen, six speakers, and 7-inch digital gauge cluster.

## 60th Anniversary Special Edition

- Limited to 2,500 U.S. vehicles; based on the Hybrid SE; arrives early 2027.
- Exclusive Ice Cap or Supersonic Red exterior, 18-inch machined black alloy wheels, and 60th Anniversary exterior badging.
- Black/Red sport-fabric seats, 10.5-inch multimedia display, anniversary door sills, and anniversary floor mats.
- MSRP had not been announced at the time of research.
