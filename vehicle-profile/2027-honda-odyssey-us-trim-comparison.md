## Powertrain and packaging

Every Odyssey uses a 280-hp, 262-lb-ft 3.5-liter V6, 10-speed automatic, and front-wheel drive. AWD and a hybrid powertrain are not offered. All seat eight and include removable Magic Slide second-row seats, a stowing 60/40 third-row Magic Seat, power sliding doors, and up to 155.8 cu ft of cargo volume with seats removed.

## Universal standard equipment

- Honda Sensing, Blind Spot Information with Cross Traffic Monitor, parking sensors, and multi-angle rearview camera.
- HondaTex or leather seating, heated power front seats, tri-zone automatic climate control, moonroof, second-row sunshades, and power tailgate.
- 9-inch touchscreen, wireless Apple CarPlay/Android Auto, wireless charging, CabinTalk, and rear-seat reminder.

## Quick buying guide

- **EX-L:** already includes the major family essentials—HondaTex, heated power seats, power doors/tailgate, moonroof, parking sensors, and Magic Slide seats.
- **Sport-L:** an appearance-focused EX-L alternative with black 19-inch wheels, dark exterior details, and red-stitched HondaTex upholstery.
- **Touring:** the road-trip trim, adding the 12.8-inch rear screen, CabinWatch, navigation, hotspot capability, and third-row sunshades.
- **Elite:** adds ventilated seats, heated steering wheel, hands-free tailgate, premium audio, rain-sensing wipers, and acoustic glass.
