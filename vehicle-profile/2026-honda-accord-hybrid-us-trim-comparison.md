> **Scope:** 2026 Accord two-motor hybrid trims. **S** = standard; **-** = not offered. MSRP excludes destination, taxes, registration, accessories, and dealer markup.

## Powertrain and performance

| System | Engine / transmission | Output | Drive |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Honda two-motor hybrid | 2.0L Atkinson-cycle four-cylinder, two electric motors, direct-drive unit | 204 total system hp; 247 lb-ft traction-motor torque | FWD only |

All trims have regenerative-braking deceleration selectors and ECON, Normal, Sport, and Individual modes. The EX-L's 17-inch wheels are the reason it earns the best EPA rating; the other hybrids use 19-inch wheels.

## Universal standard equipment

- Honda Sensing, Blind Spot Information with Cross Traffic Monitor, full LED lighting, and a multi-angle rearview camera.
- 12.3-inch touchscreen with wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, 10.2-inch digital gauge cluster, wireless phone charging, and eight-speaker audio.
- Smart Entry, remote start, dual-zone climate control, one-touch moonroof, and a 10-way power driver seat.

## Quick buying guide

- **Sport Hybrid:** lowest hybrid price and the complete large-screen technology baseline, but it omits heated seats.
- **EX-L Hybrid:** strongest rational buy—best fuel economy plus leather, heated seats, memory, and parking sensors.
- **Sport-L Hybrid:** mostly the EX-L comfort content with black 19-inch wheels and sport styling, trading away the EX-L's efficiency advantage.
- **Touring Hybrid:** adds Google built-in, Bose audio, head-up display, ventilated front seats, heated rear seats, and rain-sensing wipers.
