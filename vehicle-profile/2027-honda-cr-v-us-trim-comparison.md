# 2027 Honda CR-V (U.S.) - Gas Trim & Feature Comparison

> **Scope:** 2027 CR-V gasoline trims (LX, EX, EX-L). Hybrid trims are covered separately. **S** = standard; **A** = available; **-** = not offered. MSRP excludes destination, taxes, registration, accessories, and dealer markup.

## Lineup, price, and efficiency

| Trim | Base MSRP (FWD) | EPA MPG FWD (city/hwy/combined) | EPA MPG AWD | Positioning |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| LX | $31,520 | 28 / 33 / 30 | 27 / 31 / 29 | Entry/value |
| EX | $33,550 | 28 / 33 / 30 | 27 / 31 / 29 | Comfort/core trim |
| EX-L | $35,800 | 28 / 33 / 30 | 27 / 31 / 29 | Leather/technology |

Real Time AWD is a $1,500 option on all three trims.

## Powertrain

All gas CR-Vs use a 190-hp, 179-lb-ft turbocharged 1.5-liter four-cylinder and CVT. Normal, ECON, and Snow modes are standard. Maximum towing capacity is 1,500 lb when properly equipped.

## Universal standard equipment

- Honda Sensing, full LED headlights, multi-angle rearview camera, and 60/40 split reclining rear seat.
- 9-inch touchscreen, wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, wireless phone charger, and push-button start.
- Automatic climate control and front USB ports.

## Quick buying guide

- **LX:** retains the modern 9-inch wireless-phone interface and full core safety suite at the lowest price.
- **EX:** likely the broadest-fit gas trim, adding heated seats, power driver seat, moonroof, blind-spot monitoring, and dual-zone climate control.
- **EX-L:** adds leather, driver memory, power passenger seat/tailgate, and parking aids.
- Compare the separately covered **CR-V Hybrid** if you want stronger 204-hp output, better city economy, or the TrailSport and Sport Touring grades.
