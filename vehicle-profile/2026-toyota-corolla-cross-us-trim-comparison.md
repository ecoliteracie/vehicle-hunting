## Powertrain and performance

| System | Engine / transmission | Output | Drivetrain availability |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Gas | 2.0L four-cylinder; CVT | 169 hp | FWD standard; AWD available on L, LE, and XLE |
| Hybrid | Fifth-generation 2.0L four-cylinder Toyota Hybrid System; ECVT | 196 net combined hp | Electronic On-Demand AWD standard on Hybrid S, SE, and XSE |

- The hybrid model has a sport-tuned suspension and a manufacturer-estimated 42 MPG combined rating across its three AWD grades.
- Gas models have up to a 32 MPG combined rating. All trims have a 1,500-lb towing capacity.
- Hybrid AWD is standard equipment, not an optional upgrade; for gas models, FWD and AWD should be compared as separate in-stock configurations.

## Universal standard equipment

Every 2026 Corolla Cross includes:

- Five-passenger compact SUV body, rear-seat vents, 1,500-lb towing capacity, LED headlights/daytime running lights, and Toyota Safety Sense 3.0.
- Wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, six speakers, and Toyota Audio Multimedia.
- The gas powertrain is a 2.0L four-cylinder. Hybrid grades use the fifth-generation Toyota Hybrid System with 196 net combined hp and Electronic On-Demand AWD.

## Grade-specific highlights

- **L:** essentials-first gas grade with 8-inch screen, six speakers, 7-inch digital gauge cluster, projector LED headlights, and rear vents.
- **LE:** adds Qi charging and Blind Spot Monitor with Rear Cross-Traffic Alert.
- **XLE:** gas flagship with 18-inch wheels, standard 12.3-inch gauge cluster and 10.5-inch touchscreen, plus SofTex seating.
- **Hybrid S:** entry to the 196-hp AWD hybrid system; distinctive sport grille/bumpers, smoked Toyota emblem, black badging, matte-black heated mirrors, and 17-inch alloys.
- **Hybrid SE:** upgrades the Hybrid S with roof rails, privacy glass, sport paddle shifters, Blind Spot Monitor/RCTA, automatic climate control, Qi charging, and four USB-C ports.
- **Hybrid XSE:** top hybrid with 18-inch black wheels, SofTex/mixed-media seats, 12.3-inch gauge cluster, 10.5-inch screen, LED fog lights, 10-way power driver seat, dual-zone climate control, ambient lighting, and tonneau cover.

## Shopping notes

- Toyota states that all hybrid grades are AWD standard. Only gas grades offer a FWD/AWD choice.
- The 10.5-inch screen is standard on XLE and Hybrid XSE, and available through the Multimedia Upgrade on LE and Hybrid SE.
- A power moonroof and other equipment may depend on packages; verify a specific vehicle's window sticker.
