## Powertrain and performance

| System | Engine / transmission | Output | Drivetrain availability |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Fifth-generation Toyota Hybrid System | 2.5L four-cylinder hybrid; planetary-type ECVT | 226 net combined hp FWD; 236 net combined hp AWD | FWD on LE, SE, XLE Premium; AWD optional on those grades and standard on Woodland, XSE, Limited |

- All hybrid grades have Eco, Sport, Custom, and EV modes. EV mode is short-distance, low-speed operation when conditions permit.
- Woodland has 8.5 inches of ground clearance; LE, SE, XLE Premium, and XSE have 8.1 inches. Woodland also includes all-terrain tires and a tow hitch.
- AWD hybrid grades have a dedicated rear electric motor. XLE Premium, Woodland, SE AWD, and XSE/Limited AWD have up to 3,500-lb towing capability as specified by Toyota.

**AWD availability:** LE, SE, and XLE Premium can also be configured with Electronic On-Demand AWD. Woodland, XSE, and Limited Hybrid are AWD only. Toyota lists 45/39/42 MPG for LE/SE AWD and 45/38/41 MPG for XLE Premium AWD.

## Universal standard equipment

Every 2026 RAV4 Hybrid includes:

- Fifth-generation 2.5L four-cylinder Toyota Hybrid System: 226 net combined hp FWD or 236 hp AWD.
- 12.3-inch configurable digital gauge cluster; 10.5-inch Toyota Audio Multimedia system; wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto; five USB-C ports.
- Toyota Safety Sense 4.0, Blind Spot Monitor with Rear Cross-Traffic Alert, and backup camera with dynamic guidelines.
- Push-button start, rear-seat fold-flat function, and a rear cargo tonneau cover.

## Grade-specific highlights

- **LE:** lowest-cost Hybrid RAV4; fabric seats and the complete safety/technology baseline.
- **SE:** sport design with 18-inch black wheels, sport bumper/overfender styling, and blue interior accenting.
- **XLE Premium:** core upgrade for comfort - heated SofTex seats, 8-way power driver seat, power liftgate, 18-inch alloys, and parking assist with automatic braking.
- **Woodland:** standard AWD, all-terrain tires, 8.5-inch ground clearance, raised Woodland roof rails/crossbars, tow-hitch receiver, integrated Rigid Industries LED fog lamps, Woodland mats, and a cargo-area 120V/1,500W outlet.
- **XSE:** standard AWD sport premium grade - 20-inch black wheels, sport seating in SofTex/synthetic suede, heated/ventilated front seats, 12.9-inch screen, moonroof, and hands-free liftgate.
- **Limited:** top comfort/tech hybrid - panoramic glass roof, 9-speaker JBL audio, dual wireless chargers, rain-sensing wipers, 3D Panoramic View Monitor, Advanced Park, Front Cross-Traffic Alert, Lane Change Assist, and Traffic Jam Assist (subscription conditions apply).

## Important shopping note

Woodland, SE, and XSE also exist in the **RAV4 Plug-in Hybrid** lineup, but those are different vehicles: PHEVs are AWD-only and have different pricing, output, range, and equipment. Do not compare their window stickers directly against the hybrid prices in this file.
