# 2027 Honda HR-V (U.S.) - Trim & Feature Comparison

> **Scope:** 2027 U.S. HR-V. **S** = standard; **A** = available; **-** = not offered. MSRP excludes destination, taxes, registration, premium paint, accessories, and dealer markup.

## Lineup, price, and efficiency

| Trim | Base MSRP (FWD) | EPA MPG FWD (city/hwy/combined) | EPA MPG AWD | Positioning |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| LX | $26,600 | 26 / 32 / 28 | 25 / 30 / 27 | Entry/value |
| Sport | $28,400 | 26 / 32 / 28 | 25 / 30 / 27 | Style and cold-weather comfort |
| EX-L | $30,450 | 26 / 32 / 28 | 25 / 30 / 27 | Leather/technology |

Real Time AWD is available on every trim for $1,500.

## Powertrain

All trims use a 158-hp, 138-lb-ft naturally aspirated 2.0-liter four-cylinder and CVT. ECON, Normal, and Snow modes plus Hill Descent Control are standard. No hybrid or turbo powertrain is offered.

## Universal standard equipment

- Honda Sensing, Traffic Jam Assist, Traffic Sign Recognition, LED headlights, and rearview camera with dynamic guidelines.
- 9-inch touchscreen, wireless Apple CarPlay/Android Auto, wireless phone charger, front/console USB-C ports, and push-button start.
- 60/40 split-folding rear seats and rear privacy glass.

## Quick buying guide

- **LX:** unusually complete infotainment baseline for the price, including a 9-inch screen and wireless charging.
- **Sport:** useful everyday upgrade with blind-spot monitoring, heated seats/mirrors, remote start, Smart Entry, and 18-inch black wheels.
- **EX-L:** adds leather, power driver seat, moonroof, dual-zone climate, parking sensors, and Low-Speed Braking Control.
- AWD does not change trim equipment; it adds $1,500 and reduces the EPA combined rating by 1 MPG.
