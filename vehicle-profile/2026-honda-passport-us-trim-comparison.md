## Powertrain and capability

Every Passport has a 285-hp, 262-lb-ft 3.5-liter V6, 10-speed automatic, second-generation i-VTM4 AWD with Trail Torque Logic, seven drive modes, and 8.3 inches of ground clearance. Properly equipped towing capacity is 5,000 lb. It is a two-row, five-seat SUV; no FWD version or third row is offered.

## Universal standard equipment

- Honda Sensing, Blind Spot Information with Lane Change Assist and Cross Traffic Monitor, parking sensors, and rearview camera.
- 12.3-inch touchscreen with Google built-in, wireless Apple CarPlay/Android Auto, wireless charging, and 10.2-inch digital gauges.
- Heated front seats, power front seats with driver memory, tri-zone climate control, power tailgate, and roof rails.

## Configuration notes

- **RTL Towing** adds the factory Class III hitch and wiring harness; regular RTL retains the same 5,000-lb maximum only when properly equipped.
- **Blackout** versions are appearance configurations—black wheels, badges, and related trim—not additional mechanical capability.
- **TrailSport** is the meaningful capability step with all-terrain tires, off-road suspension, skid plates, recovery points, hitch, and TrailWatch.
- **TrailSport Elite** combines that hardware with the best comfort and audio equipment.
