Used Toyota Cars for Sale in Grapevine
We have a large selection of sedans, SUVs, and other used vehicles to meet your needs and budget. Feel free to check our website periodically to learn more about our available inventory. You can also contact us by phone or stop by the dealership to speak with a customer service representative in person to ask any questions about the vehicles on our lot. If you're looking for a car that isn't on our lot, we can use our CarFinder tool to find it.

At our Toyota dealer in Grapevine, you'll find vehicles from various quality automakers. We are confident that you will find a car that meets your needs and stays within your budget. Feel free to contact us anytime by phone or online with any questions that you might have.

Feel Good About Buying a Used Car
At Texas Toyota of Grapevine, we do whatever it takes to provide quality vehicles at affordable prices. Our service center technicians perform thorough inspections of each car that we sell before making them available to our customers. We also offer MPP service contracts and extended warranties to minimize the chances that you'll be on the hook for a significant repair bill. In the interest of transparency, we offer complimentary used car history reports on every vehicle on our lot.

Start the Process of Buying a Car Online
Our website makes it easy to start the process of evaluating your trade before you visit our location. You can also estimate your monthly payment or apply for financing from the privacy of your own home. There are a variety of financing options through our trusted lending partners to help ensure that your vehicle payment fits within your budget. A representative from our finance department is standing by to answer any questions that you might have about the process of obtaining a loan.

Used Cars for Sale Nearby
If you're shopping for used Toyota cars for sale in the Grapevine area, including Dallas, Fort Worth, and Plano, we'd love to hear from you. Our service team is committed to creating a comfortable and enjoyable buying experience. You can schedule a test drive or ask any questions that you might have about our inventory by calling our location during regular business hours. Alternatively, you can stop by our Grapevine Toyota dealership to speak to someone in person.

Purchase prices do not include tax, title and license. $225 Doc Fee is included in the advertised price. Optional equipment and upgrades may be offered at time of sale for additional cost or removed by the dealer for no additional cost. Get Today's Price is available to all customers and can also be obtained by calling or coming into the dealership today. Prices include the listed Factory Rebates and Incentives. Pricing also includes dealer adds. Please verify all information. We are not responsible for typographical, technical, or misprint errors. Inventory is subject to prior sale. Contact us via phone or email for more details.
